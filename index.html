D1 Reading single candles

Chart drill (after episode 2)

  1. 1 = a long-wick rejection; 2 = a doji (indecision); 3 = a strong close.
  2. The highs were rejected: buyers couldn't hold higher (weakness).
  3. Each candle is one clue; you need trend, structure and a plan.

D2 Name the trend

Chart drill (after episode 4)

  1. A = downtrend; B = consolidation; C = uptrend.
  2. Uptrend: the latest higher low. Downtrend: the latest lower high.
  3. Buy pullbacks in the uptrend, sell rallies in the downtrend, wait in the range.

D3 Real break or fake-out?

Chart drill (after episode 6)

  1. A: YES, break to the downside: a body closed below the level.
  2. B: NO. Wick poked through, closed back inside: a sweep.
  3. C: YES, break to the upside: a body closed above the level.
  4. D: NO. Wick poked through, closed back inside: a sweep.
  5. E: YES, break to the downside: a body closed below the level.
  6. F: NO. Wick poked through, closed back inside: a sweep.

D4 Find the sweep

Chart drill (after episode 10)

  1. A: equal highs swept; BOS closed below the level; expect price to go down toward the opposite low.
  2. B: equal lows swept; BOS closed above the level; expect price to go up toward the opposite high.
  3. C: equal highs swept; BOS closed below the level; expect price to go down toward the opposite low.
  4. D: equal lows swept; BOS closed above the level; expect price to go up toward the opposite high.

D5 Which gaps are real?

Chart drill (after episode 16)

  1. 1A: NOT valid, the wicks of candles 1 and 3 overlap.
  2. 1B: valid bullish FVG (candle 1 high to candle 3 low).
  3. 1C: valid bearish FVG (candle 1 low to candle 3 high).
  4. 2A: valid bullish FVG (candle 1 high to candle 3 low).
  5. 2B: NOT valid, the wicks of candles 1 and 3 overlap.
  6. 2C: valid bearish FVG (candle 1 low to candle 3 high).

D6 Box the order block

Chart drill (after episode 22)

  1. A (bearish): the up-leg that swept the high before the break; wait for a pullback up into it, then a lower-timeframe BOS down.
  2. B (bullish): the down-leg that swept the low; wait for a pullback down into it, then a lower-timeframe BOS up.
  3. Stop beyond the sweep; target the previous liquidity.

D7 Premium or discount?

Chart drill (after episode 26)

  1. A: low 99.7, high 113.7, equilibrium 106.7. Levels: A=110.2, B=106.7, C=103.2. Bullish swing: buy from the discount: C.
  2. B: low 99.7, high 113.7, equilibrium 106.7. Levels: A=103.2, B=106.7, C=110.2. Bearish swing: sell from the premium: C.

D8 Everything together

Chart drill (after episode 24)

  1. A (sell): liquidity above the highs swept (1); a close below the level breaks structure (2); pullback into the FVG inside the block (3); stop above the sweep; target the previous low.
  2. B (buy): the mirror image.
  3. Plan: bias, sweep, BOS, pullback into OB/FVG, smaller-timeframe confirmation with defined stop and target.

Extra practice (not from the videos)

  1. UP. Good for the left (base) currency: pair rises.
  2. DOWN. Good for the right (quote) currency: pair falls.
  3. UP. USD is on the left: pair rises.
  4. DOWN. JPY is on the right: pair falls.
  5. DOWN. Bad for the base currency: pair falls.
  6. UP. Bad for the quote currency: pair rises.
  7. DOWN (bias). The instructor reads USD strength as bearish for both; price may disagree, so wait.
  8. SKIP. Sit out these four events entirely.
